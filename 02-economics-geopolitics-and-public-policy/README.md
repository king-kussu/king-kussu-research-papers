# Economics, Geopolitics & Public Policy

## KING KUSSU — Independent Research

This collection presents independent research by KING KUSSU examining economic policy, sovereign debt, fiscal sustainability, international relations, political sovereignty, geopolitical power, and the long-term economic consequences of major policy decisions.

## Research Publications

### 1. The Economic Stakes of a United Kingdom Withdrawal from the European Union

**Author:** KING KUSSU

This research examines the economic and strategic consequences of the United Kingdom's withdrawal from the European Union, including financial services, labor mobility, exchange rates, economic interdependence, and international bargaining. The historical analysis is accompanied by a retrospective assessment.

### 2. The Financial Limits of Global Power: America's $40 Trillion Debt

**Author:** KING KUSSU  
**Year:** 2026

This paper examines United States federal debt, fiscal sustainability, military expenditure, monetary supremacy, and the relationship between economic capacity and geopolitical influence. It also considers historical comparisons involving the rise and decline of great powers.
