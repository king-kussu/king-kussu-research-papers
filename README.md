### 01 — Artificial Intelligence, Technology & Governance

Research examining artificial intelligence, algorithmic decision-making, technological power, human sovereignty, institutional accountability, and the governance of emerging computational systems.

#### 1. The Algorithmic Executive

**Subtitle:** Decision Authority and Human Sovereignty in the Age of Artificial Intelligence

**Author:** KING KUSSU  
**Year:** 2026  
**Length:** 17 pages  
**Research type:** Conceptual synthesis and documentary analysis

This paper examines how artificial intelligence can influence consequential institutional decisions before formal human approval occurs.

It investigates the distinction between formal authority and operational influence, identifies mechanisms through which practical decision-making power can shift toward algorithmic systems, and develops a framework for preserving meaningful human control.

The research introduces six institutional capacities associated with human sovereignty, including legitimate purpose, independent knowledge, effective contestation, timely intervention, accountable responsibility, and continuity with credible exit.

### 04 — Philosophy, Consciousness & Civilizational Studies

Research exploring philosophical truth, consciousness, epistemology, ethical responsibility, civilizational identity, sovereignty, cultural memory, institutional resilience, and collective continuity.

#### 7. Truth Does Not Contradict Itself

**Subtitle:** The Permanence of Truth, the Limits of Human Perception, and the Ethics of Living Truthfully

**Author:** KING KUSSU  
**Document year:** 2009  
**Length:** 20 pages  
**Research type:** Interdisciplinary philosophical and conceptual inquiry

This paper examines the proposition that truth does not contradict itself and investigates the distinction between objective reality and human perception, belief, memory, interpretation, and knowledge.

The research explores epistemology, ontology, the principle of non-contradiction, cognitive bias, motivated reasoning, self-deception, intellectual humility, and the ethical responsibility to pursue truthfulness.

It introduces epistemic integrity as a commitment to aligning evidence, belief, confidence, language, and conduct while remaining open to correction.

#### 8. The Living Civilisation

**Subtitle:** The Anatomy of Civilizational Survival: Identity, Sovereignty, Cohesion, and Continuity

**Author:** KING KUSSU  
**Year:** 2026  
**Length:** 20 pages  
**Research type:** Interdisciplinary conceptual and systems-based inquiry

This paper develops the Civilizational Vitality Framework to examine how societies preserve historical identity, cultural memory, institutional coherence, sovereignty, resilience, and continuity across generations.

Using a carefully qualified analogy between living systems and civilizations, the research investigates the interdependence of governance, security, economic capacity, communication, cultural inheritance, institutional repair, social cohesion, and adaptation.

The paper emphasizes that civilizational continuity requires both preservation and responsible change, while rejecting biological interpretations of human communities and exclusionary applications of the organism analogy.
